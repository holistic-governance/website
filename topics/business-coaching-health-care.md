# Business Coaching for Health Care Businesses

> Holistic Governance offers one-to-one business coaching for owners, founders and leaders of health care businesses. The coach is Naomi Alefelder, a registered nurse, lead auditor and board director with an MBA and more than 10 years in health care, including senior governance, quality and risk leadership. Coaching supports the decisions the business makes; it does not guarantee any business, compliance or accreditation outcome.

## Who is the coaching for?

Owners and leaders of health care businesses, including:

- general practice and primary care clinics;
- allied health and community health practices;
- mental health services;
- day procedure services and private hospitals;
- residential aged care and home care providers;
- disability (NDIS) providers.

It suits businesses that are starting up, growing, adding a new service line, or preparing for registration, a tender or an audit, and owners who want a sounding board who knows both business and health care regulation.

## What does the coaching cover?

1. **Growth and strategy** — business planning, new services and service lines, pricing and positioning.
2. **Governance, risk and quality set-up** — boards and advisory structures, policies, risk and quality systems that grow with the business rather than being bolted on before an audit.
3. **Regulatory readiness** — getting ready to register, tender or be audited, for example against the Strengthened Aged Care Quality Standards, the NDIS Practice Standards or the NSQHS Standards. See the related references: [Aged care provider registration and audits](https://hg-au.com/topics/acqs-accreditation-audit.md), [NDIS provider audits and certification](https://hg-au.com/topics/ndis-provider-audits-certification.md), [NSQHS Standards](https://hg-au.com/topics/nsqhs-standards.md).
4. **Leadership and operations** — leadership habits, team structure, workflows and reporting for owners and managers.

## How does it work?

- One-to-one with the owner or leader. Length and rhythm are agreed at the start, around the business's needs.
- Goals are set by the client and worked on through the real decisions in front of the business.
- Coaching is not legal, financial or clinical advice; where those are needed, the coach will say so.

## How is business coaching different from governance mentoring?

| | Business coaching | Governance mentoring |
|---|---|---|
| For | Owners and leaders of the business | Professionals in quality, risk, compliance or clinical governance roles |
| Focus | The business: strategy, growth, systems, readiness, operations | The person: capability and confidence in a governance role |
| Reference | This document | [Governance Mentoring for Health Care Professionals](https://hg-au.com/topics/governance-mentoring-health-care.md) |
