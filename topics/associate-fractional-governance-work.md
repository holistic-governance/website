# Associate, Subcontract and Fractional Governance Work in Health Care

> Holistic Governance takes on associate and subcontract work with consultancies, audit firms and health care providers that need senior clinical governance and quality capacity for a project or a set period. It also provides interim and fractional quality leadership. The work is led by Naomi Alefelder, a registered nurse, lead auditor and board director. Conflicts of interest are checked before any assignment is accepted.

## What do the terms mean?

- **Associate or subcontract work:** a consultancy, audit firm or provider brings Holistic Governance in to deliver part of an engagement, for a project or a set period.
- **Interim leadership:** an acting quality, clinical governance, compliance or executive lead, including General Manager or CEO, during recruitment, leave or an organisational transition.
- **Fractional leadership:** a senior quality, risk, General Manager or CEO role held part-time on an ongoing basis, for organisations that need the expertise but not a full-time role.

## Who is it for?

- **Consultancies and audit firms** that need extra senior clinical governance capacity for a project.
- **Hospitals and day procedure services** preparing for accreditation or covering a vacant quality role.
- **Aged care and disability providers** that need quality and risk leadership without a full-time appointment.
- **Digital health companies** that need clinical and AI governance advice.

## What work is offered?

- Interim or fractional quality and risk lead roles, for leave cover, vacancies or accreditation preparation.
- Interim or fractional General Manager and CEO roles.
- General and clinical management support.
- Mock accreditation audits, gap analysis and internal audit against the NSQHS, Aged Care Quality and NDIS Practice Standards.
- Governance framework and committee reviews.
- Policy and procedure review and writing.
- Corrective action and remediation plans.
- Board quality reporting, including Power BI dashboards.
- Clinical and AI governance advice for digital health companies — see [Clinical AI Governance](https://hg-au.com/topics/clinical-ai-governance.md).

## How does it work?

- **Scope:** a project or a set period, agreed before work starts.
- **Delivery:** remote or on site.
- **Conflicts of interest:** checked before any assignment is accepted.
