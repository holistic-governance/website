# Governance Mentoring for Health Care Professionals

> The HG Mentoring Program is one-to-one mentoring from Holistic Governance for professionals in any health care sector who are stepping into a quality, risk, compliance or clinical governance role, or building governance capability. The mentor is Naomi Alefelder, a registered nurse, lead auditor and board director with more than 10 years in health care. Mentoring supports a person's development and does not guarantee any outcome, compliance result or accreditation decision.

## Who is the mentoring for?

Professionals across all health care sectors, including:

- **Hospitals and day procedure services** — quality managers, clinical governance leads, accreditation coordinators.
- **Primary care and general practice** — practice managers and nurses leading accreditation, quality improvement or risk.
- **Mental health services** — quality and safety leads in public, private and community managed services.
- **Aged care** — residential and home care quality, compliance and clinical governance roles.
- **Disability services** — NDIS quality, compliance and practice leads.
- **Community health** — quality, risk and compliance staff in community and allied health services.

It suits people who are:

- new to a quality, risk or compliance role, including clinicians moving into governance work;
- the only quality or risk person in their organisation and without a peer to test ideas with;
- preparing for an accreditation, certification or regulatory audit for the first time;
- building governance capability in a team, where the organisation funds mentoring for a staff member.

## What standards does the mentor work with?

Mentees work on the standards that apply to their own service. These include:

- National Safety and Quality Health Service (NSQHS) Standards — hospitals and day procedure services.
- National Safety and Quality Primary and Community Healthcare Standards.
- RACGP Standards for general practices (5th edition).
- National Safety and Quality Mental Health Standards for Community Managed Organisations, and the National Safety and Quality Digital Mental Health Standards.
- Strengthened Aged Care Quality Standards.
- NDIS Practice Standards.
- The 2026 National Model for Clinical Governance — see [National Model for Clinical Governance](https://hg-au.com/topics/national-model-clinical-governance.md).

## How does the program work?

- **Format:** one-to-one. Program length and session rhythm are set with the mentee, around their needs.
- **Path:** contract (agree purpose and boundaries) → diagnose (self-assess and set clear goals) → develop (work on live challenges) → review (check progress and adjust) → close (consolidate and plan ahead).
- **Goals** are set by the mentee and worked on through real challenges from their role, such as incidents, policy, audit, risk and reporting.

## What does the employer see?

When an organisation funds mentoring for a staff member:

| The organisation sees | Stays confidential |
|---|---|
| Sessions held | What is said in sessions |
| Goals and ratings the mentee chooses to share | Notes, actions and worksheets |
| A closing summary in the mentee's words | Anything the mentee has not approved |

Confidentiality has written limits, agreed at the start.
