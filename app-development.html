Common Questions

Prototype build FAQs

What is a prototype build?
A working prototype of your app, dashboard or workflow tool — real screens and real user journeys, not static mockups — designed around how your team actually works, so you can test the idea with users, your board or investors before committing to full development.
What do we get at the end?
The working prototype itself, plus a handover pack and a build-ready roadmap — so whichever way your decision goes, you keep the thinking.
Is a prototype the same as an MVP or a proof of concept?
The terms overlap. A proof of concept shows something is feasible; an MVP is a first releasable product. Our prototypes sit between the two: working software real users can trial, built to de-risk the investment decision without the cost of production engineering.
What happens if we decide to go ahead with a full build?
The prototype work carries forward — the mapped workflow, validated user journeys and build-ready roadmap give whichever development team you engage a tested starting point rather than a blank page.
